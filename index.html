        The research behind these ideas

        Groups with a research note lean on what graduates say they want. NACE covers the practical side well. Deloitte covers purpose and values, which NACE does not report on. Groups without a note do not have a survey behind them yet.

        • NACE, Beyond the Paycheck: What the Class of 2026 Really Wants from an Employer (June 3, 2026). From the NACE 2026 Student Survey: more than 17,000 students at 258 schools, including more than 1,860 graduating seniors, surveyed March 12 to May 15, 2026.
        • NACE, Opportunity to Grow, Advance Tops Wish List for Class of 2026 (May 15, 2026). Same survey, with more on what graduating seniors rank highest.
        • NACE, Job Stability, Healthy Workplace Culture Top New Grad Wish List (May 19, 2025). From the NACE 2024 Student Survey: more than 2,900 juniors, surveyed March 13 to May 15, 2024.
        • Deloitte, 2025 Gen Z and Millennial Survey. 23,482 respondents in 44 countries, surveyed October 25 to December 24, 2024.
